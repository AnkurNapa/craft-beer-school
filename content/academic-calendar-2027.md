# Craft Beer School: Academic Calendar 2027

**Planning draft | January–December 2027 | India Standard Time**

## Delivery assumptions

- One batch of every one of the 27 catalogue online courses. Additional offerings appear below.
- Online teaching: Saturday and Sunday, 12–2 pm IST. Each listed weekend includes both days: four contact hours. Duration means teaching weeks; breaks extend the elapsed calendar period.
- Three parallel tracks require up to three separate instructors and online rooms. Students cannot attend simultaneous courses across tracks. Instructor availability has not been confirmed.
- Catalogue course lengths are retained. Total: 71 course teaching weeks, 142 sessions and 284 contact hours across the school.
- Maximum 20 students per online batch, per existing school notes. This is a proposed 2027 schedule, not an announced intake.
- Dates without assigned teaching in a track are available for tutorials, replacement classes and marking. Do not automatically fill festival closures.

## Weekday holidays and regional adjustments

Keep weekday events and deadlines off Republic Day (26 January), Eid-ul-Fitr (10 March, tentative), Holi (23 March in the government-based list), Ugadi/Gudi Padwa (7 April), Vaisakhi/Vishu (14 April), Ram Navami (15 April), Mahavir Jayanti (19 April), Eid al-Adha (17 May, tentative), Buddha Purnima (20 May), Muharram (16 June, tentative), Rath Yatra (5 July), Raksha Bandhan (17 August), Janmashtami (25 August) and the October/November festival dates covered by the breaks above. [Holiday source](https://aiimsrishikesh.edu.in/images/upload_documents/O.M.%20No.%201351%20dated%2005.09.2026.pdf).

Before opening enrolment, check the applicable 2027 state calendars for each venue and the enrolled cohort. Regional observances can require another replacement weekend. Islamic dates remain subject to local confirmation. During fasting periods, offer a non-tasting assignment or a later tasting date.

The secondary calendar lists Holi on 22 March whereas the government-based list uses 23 March. Keep both days clear of deadlines. [Cross-check calendar](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/india/2027).

## Assessment and administration

- Open registration approximately six weeks before each course and close it one week before its first class; adjust administrative deadlines for holidays.
- Assign practice work after each teaching weekend. Set submissions on the next teaching weekend, extending for observance-related needs.
- Hold the final presentation in the last listed class. Allow up to two subsequent non-closure weeks for marking and certificate administration, with extensions where necessary.
- Existing completion standard: all assignments submitted and at least 80% live attendance. Recordings support catch-up; confirm attendance exceptions before enrolment.
- Use available track weekends for replacement teaching without reducing advertised contact hours.
