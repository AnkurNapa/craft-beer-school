Syllabus

Week by week.

8 weeks, two live sessions every weekend, one assignment a week.

Week 01. How styles work

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • BJCP and Brewers Association guidelines
  • Reading a style sheet: OG, FG, IBU, colour and ABV ranges
  • How history, water and tax shaped styles

Assignment: Profile one style of your choice against its guideline.

Week 02. Lagers

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Helles, Pilsner, Märzen and Dunkel
  • Cold fermentation, diacetyl rest and lagering

Assignment: Write a Pilsner recipe with a full fermentation schedule.

Week 03. Wheat beers

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Hefeweizen and witbier
  • The ferulic acid rest, clove and banana
  • Coriander and orange peel without overdoing them

Assignment: Design a wheat beer and justify every spice and yeast choice.

Week 04. British ales

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Bitters, milds and porters
  • English yeast, crystal malts and cask conditioning

Assignment: Build a best bitter at under 4% ABV that still has body.

Week 05. IPAs

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • English, West Coast and New England
  • Hop schedules and dry hopping
  • Oxygen, haze and hop creep

Assignment: Write two IPA recipes from the same base malt.

Week 06. Stouts and dark beers

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Dry, sweet and imperial stouts
  • Roasted barley, coffee and chocolate
  • Nitro dispense

Assignment: Create a stout and plan how you would serve it.

Week 07. Belgian ales

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Saison, dubbel and tripel
  • Warm fermentation, phenols and candi sugar

Assignment: Write a saison fermentation plan and predict its flavour.

Week 08. Sours and competition brewing

2 live sessions, Saturday and Sunday

  • Kettle sours and mixed fermentation
  • Judging sheets and how beers are scored
  • Picking and entering a competition

Assignment: Submit a competition brew plan and get it critiqued.
