  • What exactly is beer?

  • Why does beer have foam?
  • How is beer made?
  • Why does one beer feel light and another rich and full-bodied?

What you get

What you walk away with.

  • A plain-English picture of what beer is and how it is made
  • The difference between lager and ale plus the main styles by name
  • A simple tasting method you can use on the next beer you order
  • Something worth saying at the table besides which brand you like
The hour

Sixty minutes, start to finish.

Live and visual, with time for your questions at the end. Session dates for each batch are shared when you enrol.

0 to 10 min. What beer is
Malt, hops, water and yeast: what each one brings to the glass.
10 to 20 min. How beer is made
Grain to glass in one picture: mash, boil, ferment, condition, package.
20 to 32 min. Lager, ale and the main styles
Why yeast decides the family, then a quick tour from pale lager to stout.
32 to 42 min. Craft and commercial beer
What the word craft means and why two pale lagers can taste nothing alike.
42 to 52 min. How to taste a beer
Look, smell, sip: bitterness, sweetness, body and why the foam matters.
52 to 60 min. Your questions and what next
Beer next to wine and spirits, your questions answered live and where to go from here.
