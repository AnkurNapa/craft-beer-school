Syllabus

Week by week.

2 weeks. Two live sessions every weekend and one assignment a week.

Week 01. How tasting works
  • Aroma, appearance, flavour and mouthfeel
  • Glassware, serving temperature and tasting order
  • Flavour chemistry and the flavour wheel

Assignment: Taste a flight at home and describe each beer in writing.

Week 02. Off-flavours and scoring
  • Diacetyl, DMS, acetaldehyde, oxidation, light-struck, phenolic and sulphur
  • Where each fault comes from and how to fix it
  • Triangle tests and BJCP-style scoresheets

Assignment: Score a flight and compare your sheet with the instructor's.
