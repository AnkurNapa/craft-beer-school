Before you go

Hop safely.

  • Never drive. Book a cab or use the metro between stops, and plan the ride home first.
  • Order tasters or a sampler paddle, not full pints, at every stop.
  • A glass of water between beers and food at the first stop.
  • Carry ID: the legal drinking age differs between states.
  • Call ahead: hours, holidays and dry days change, and venues open and close.
