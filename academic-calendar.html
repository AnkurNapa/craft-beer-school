Academic Calendar 2027

Every weekend of 2027, planned.

All 27 online courses in one year, taught on Saturdays and Sundays from 12 to 2 pm IST. This is a proposed schedule, not an announced intake.

How it works

Three tracks, one weekend slot.

Each class weekend is two days of two hours, so four contact hours. 27 courses add up to 71 teaching weeks. Batches take up to 20 students. Recordings help you catch up. To complete a course, submit every assignment and attend at least 80% of live classes.
