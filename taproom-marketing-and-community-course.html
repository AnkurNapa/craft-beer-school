Syllabus

Week by week.

2 weeks. Two live sessions every weekend and one assignment a week.

Week 01. Bringing people back
  • Why first visits do not become regulars
  • Events that fit your beer and your space
  • Beer clubs, loyalty and WhatsApp groups
  • Responsible serving at every event

Assignment: Plan a three-month taproom event calendar.

Week 02. People and partners
  • Staff as the brand: hiring, training and service
  • Tap list talk that helps guests choose
  • Local partners, collaborations and food pairings
  • Measuring footfall and repeat visits

Assignment: Write a one-page service guide and a plan for one partnership.
