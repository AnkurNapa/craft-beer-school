Questions

Before you buy.

Which brewing yeast copes best with Indian heat?

Kveik strains are the ones whose makers publish ranges above 30 °C. In this guide: LalBrew Belle Saison (Lallemand, up to 35 °C), LalBrew Voss (Lallemand, up to 40 °C), WLP518 Opshaug Kveik Ale (White Labs, up to 35 °C), Omega OYL-071 Lutra Kveik (Omega, up to 35 °C). Clean American ale strains such as US-05 still need controlled fermentation around 18 to 22 °C for a clean result.

Dry or liquid yeast for brewing in India?

Dry yeast tolerates shipping and storage far better, which matters when the cold chain is uncertain. Liquid yeast offers more strains but should travel and stay cold, and be used quickly.

What does STA1 positive mean?

The strain carries a gene that lets it break down dextrins most yeasts leave behind, so it can ferment drier and keep fermenting in the package. Keep it on separate equipment from your other beers.
