Questions

Everything you wanted to ask.

Courses, format, certification, payment and refunds, answered.

Do I need any brewing experience to start?

Not at all. Brewing Fundamentals is built for complete beginners, we start with the science and ingredients and build up from there. Many of our students had no prior brewing background.

Are the courses online or in person?

Both. Our six core courses run as flexible live online sessions you can join from any city. We also offer in-person workshops, home-visit brewing and guided tastings, mainly around Bengaluru.

What are the class sizes?

Small by design. Tiny cohorts mean every question gets answered and every batch gets tasted, with one-on-one mentorship from industry experts.

Do you help with WSET or Cicerone certification?

Yes. We provide structured exam preparation for WSET and Cicerone so you can build globally recognised beer knowledge and sit the exams with confidence.

How do I enrol and pay?

Pick a course and submit the enquiry form or WhatsApp us. We confirm dates and share payment details. Seats are confirmed once payment is received, cohorts fill quickly.

What is your refund policy?

See our full Refund & Cancellation policy. In short, cancellations before a cohort starts are eligible for a refund within the stated window.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, courses include a digital certificate and access to the Craft Beer School community.

Can you teach a group or corporate session?

Absolutely. We run private workshops and tastings for teams and events. Reach out via the contact page with your group size and dates.
